Amazon is not optional anymore

📦
Patrick Le-Tran Front Row · 5 min read
eCommerce Product

Malls are dying. Foot traffic is down. And yet, more money is moving through ecommerce than ever before. If you sell a physical product and you're not thinking seriously about where people are actually shopping, you're already behind.

Spending two years working in ecommerce full time has given me a pretty clear view of where the industry is headed. The shift away from brick-and-mortar isn't a trend anymore. It's the baseline. Consumers want speed, convenience, and instant gratification, and online shopping delivers all three. If your brand sells a physical product, having a strong ecommerce presence isn't a nice-to-have. It's the foundation.

A lot of people hear "ecommerce" and immediately think direct-to-consumer — a slick Shopify store with good branding and a solid email list. And that matters. But what I've learned working at Front Row is that Amazon is the real engine. It's one of the highest-traffic retail platforms on the internet, and it already handles the hard part: distribution, fulfillment, and a built-in customer base that's ready to buy. If you're not on Amazon, you're leaving volume on the table.

The catch is that Amazon is genuinely complex. It's not a platform you just set up and forget. There's SEO copywriting, keyword strategy, listing optimization, logistics, and the fundamental question of whether to go first-party or third-party — each with real tradeoffs in margin, control, and operational lift. Getting it right takes expertise, which is exactly why agencies and consultants like Front Row exist. Brands that want to scale on Amazon need partners who understand the platform deeply, not just the surface level.

The value being poured into ecommerce is enormous, and most people still underestimate it. If you're building or growing a physical product brand, understanding Amazon isn't just useful. It's essential.
